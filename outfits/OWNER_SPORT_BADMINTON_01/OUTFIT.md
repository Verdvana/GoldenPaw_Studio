## Silhouette and fit

Athletic court outfit with free shoulder/arm movement and a practical tennis-skirt silhouette. The visor has no fabric over the crown: only a narrow approximately two-finger-width band around the head and a projecting front brim. The top and skirt use new silhouettes; the skirt is pure white. Socks use white 15D matte hosiery texture rather than ribbed knit. Shoes remain light-colored and purpose-built for badminton.
