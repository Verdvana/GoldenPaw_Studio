## Silhouette and fit

Youthful winter office silhouette for a pear-shaped heroine: a soft-mist-pink fitted knit top tucked into a high-waisted charcoal-gray skirt with only a gentle A-line flare, under a short light-oat wool jacket. Keep the upper-body line clean and slightly structured while avoiding bulk at the hips. The skirt hem sits approximately 6 cm above the knee to preserve leg proportion. The hosiery must be visibly thicker 50D, lightly translucent with subtle sheen and some natural skin depth. Confirm jacket hem, skirt flare, and boot details.
