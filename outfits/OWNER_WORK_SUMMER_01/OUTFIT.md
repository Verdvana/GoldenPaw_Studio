Fitted summer office silhouette with a lower tasteful neckline. Skirt ends at mid-thigh, has a rear slit, and uses a champagne-ivory satin base with dense small black polka dots. Footwear uses a softly round-pointed toe, a clean curved-cut vamp with subtle side opening, a single straight rear slingback strap, and an approximately 5 cm stable kitten heel.
