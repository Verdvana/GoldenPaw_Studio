## Silhouette and fit

Athletic court outfit with free shoulder/arm movement and a practical tennis-skirt silhouette. The visor has no fabric over the crown: only a narrow pale mint-green band around the head and a projecting white front brim. The sleeveless tank is very pale mint green. The skirt is pure white with built-in shorts. Socks use white 15D matte hosiery texture rather than ribbed knit. Shoes are white with small pale mint and soft lavender accents and a court-appropriate performance sole.
