The absolute front of every visible foot must be seamless and uniform: show no visible toe seam, horizontal stitching, darker transverse line, reinforced band, piping, cord or raised ridge across the toe tips. A continuous translucent velvet-matte nylon membrane must extend from the ankle over the foot top and all five toes as one laterally tensioned, highly elastic sheet. At every gap between adjacent toes, the nylon must span across the opening in a smooth, shallow, gently concave tension arc; it must not sink or wedge deeply into the toe cleft, cling tightly around each separate toe, or resemble individually wrapped glove fingers. No skin gap may appear exposed. The same nude-beige density and fine micro-weave must continue without fading from ankle to foot top to toe tips. The grouped toe contours and nail edges must look gently compressed, softened and hazy below the fabric, never sharply separated like bare toes. Deep-red toenail polish must appear only as muted burgundy shapes underneath the nylon veil, with no crisp naked nail edges and no direct glossy nail highlights. In video, preserve the seamless front edge, continuous spanning membrane, shallow inter-toe tension arcs, weave, diffusion and muted nail color consistently in every frame; they must never flicker, vanish, develop a toe seam, sink into the toe clefts, become individually toe-wrapped or toe-less hosiery, or turn into bare feet after motion or occlusion. Frame visible feet large and clear enough for this construction evidence to remain readable. Any visible toe that reads as bare skin, any visible front toe seam, or any tight glove-like toe-by-toe wrapping is a failed result and must be rejected.

In video, preserve the same clearly recognizable muted deep-red polish beneath the nylon on every anatomically visible dorsal nail plate in every frame without flicker, fading, color shift, disappearance, or bare-nail exposure.
