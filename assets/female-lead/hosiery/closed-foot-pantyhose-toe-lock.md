# Closed-foot pantyhose toe lock｜必须逐字加入实际生成提示词

NON-NEGOTIABLE CLOSED-FOOT PANTYHOSE TOE LOCK: Whenever her toes are visible through open-toe shoes or slippers, the pantyhose must remain unmistakably present over the entire foot and every toe. The absolute front of every visible foot must be seamless and uniform: show no visible toe seam, horizontal stitching, darker transverse line, reinforced band, piping, cord or raised ridge across the toe tips. A continuous translucent velvet-matte nylon membrane must extend from the ankle over the foot top and all five toes as one laterally tensioned, highly elastic sheet. At every gap between adjacent toes, the nylon must span across the opening in a smooth, shallow, gently concave tension arc; it must not sink or wedge deeply into the toe cleft, cling tightly around each separate toe, or resemble individually wrapped glove fingers. No skin gap may appear exposed. The same nude-beige density and fine micro-weave must continue without fading from ankle to foot top to toe tips. The grouped toe contours and nail edges must look gently compressed, softened and hazy below the fabric, never sharply separated like bare toes. Deep-red toenail polish must appear only as muted burgundy shapes underneath the nylon veil, with no crisp naked nail edges and no direct glossy nail highlights. In video, preserve the seamless front edge, continuous spanning membrane, shallow inter-toe tension arcs, weave, diffusion and muted nail color consistently in every frame; they must never flicker, vanish, develop a toe seam, sink into the toe clefts, become individually toe-wrapped or toe-less hosiery, or turn into bare feet after motion or occlusion. Frame visible feet large and clear enough for this construction evidence to remain readable. Any visible toe that reads as bare skin, any visible front toe seam, or any tight glove-like toe-by-toe wrapping is a failed result and must be rejected.

NON-NEGOTIABLE DEEP-RED TOENAIL VISIBILITY LOCK: All five toenails on every visible foot are painted deep red before the pantyhose is worn. The polish must remain clearly and immediately recognizable as five distinct muted deep-red or burgundy color shapes seen through the continuous nylon veil. Hosiery diffusion may soften nail edges and suppress glossy highlights, but it must never erase, neutralize, desaturate, obscure, or make the red polish too faint to identify. Do not show unpainted, nude, pale-pink, beige, colorless, or missing toenails. Do not show bare glossy polish above the hosiery. If the viewer cannot readily tell that the toenails are painted deep red beneath the pantyhose, the result is failed and must be rejected. In video, preserve the same clearly recognizable muted deep-red polish beneath the nylon on every visible toenail in every frame without flicker, fading, color shift, disappearance, or bare-nail exposure.
