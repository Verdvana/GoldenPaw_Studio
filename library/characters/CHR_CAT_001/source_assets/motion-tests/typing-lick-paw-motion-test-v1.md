## 时间设计

| 时间 | 动作 |
|---|---|
| 0.0–0.7秒 | 猫咪坐在MacBook Pro前，双爪悬停在键盘上，眼睛专注屏幕。 |
| 0.7–2.5秒 | 双爪高速交替敲击键盘，爪子产生轻微自然运动模糊，身体和头部保持稳定，尾巴尖略微抬起。 |
| 2.5–3.1秒 | 动作突然停止，一只前爪抬起，眼睛短暂向侧面看。 |
| 3.1–4.1秒 | 慢条斯理地舔一次抬起的前爪，神情严肃，另一只爪留在键盘旁。 |
| 4.1–5.0秒 | 前爪回到键盘，恢复高速输入，尾巴尖慢慢落回原位。 |

## 视频生成提示词

Create a single continuous five-second cinematic character animation using the locked identity image as the highest-priority identity reference and the six-panel keyframe sheet as timing and motion reference. The exact same chubby five-year-old male golden-apricot British Shorthair cat sits at the same open space-black MacBook Pro for the entire shot. Fixed cat-eye-level three-quarter camera, no cuts, no zoom, no camera movement. Warm home-office daylight and subtle cool screen reflection remain constant.

The cat begins with both paws poised above the keyboard, then types rapidly and precisely with alternating front-paw taps. The paws show restrained natural motion blur while the round head and chubby body stay steady. The thick tail tip rises slightly. At 2.5 seconds he stops abruptly, raises one front paw, glances sideways with a serious blank face, then calmly licks that paw once as if pretending nothing happened. At 4.1 seconds the paw returns to the keyboard and rapid typing resumes through the end.

Preserve the exact locked identity throughout: broad near-circular face, full cheeks, large round grey-green eyes with dark rims, small dusty-pink nose, cream-white muzzle and chest, warm saturated golden-apricot plush short fur, compact chubby adult body and thick fluffy tail. Realistic feline anatomy and photorealistic fur with subtle animated-feature acting. Keep the MacBook Pro, desk, background, framing, lighting and shadows unchanged throughout.

Avoid identity drift, face morphing, coat-color changes, camera movement, scene changes, laptop deformation, human fingers, long claws, floating paws, extra limbs, extra cats, talking mouth, exaggerated tongue, broad smile, Vision Pro, text, subtitles and watermark.
