# CHR_CAT_001 生产身份提示词 v001

## 用途

用于生成中性身份母版及基础转面。实际生成时还需要明确目标角度，并选择正面身份母版与最接近角度的辅助参考图。

## 正向身份描述

```text
The exact same fictional five-year-old male golden-shaded British Shorthair cat, a large sturdy chubby but agile real domestic cat. Extremely broad near-circular face, very full rounded cheeks, large round gray-green to olive-green eyes with natural dark rims and large pupils, eyes set relatively low and wide, small dusty-pink nose, short cream-white muzzle and chin, upright triangular ears with rounded tips, warm golden-apricot plush short coat with subtle darker golden shading on forehead and back, cream muzzle, throat and chest, compact broad adult body, short sturdy legs, round natural paws, thick fluffy golden tail with a softly transitioning moderately darker warm brown-gold tip. Photorealistic feline anatomy and natural individual fur.
```

## 固定生成环境

```text
Seamless neutral light-gray photography studio, clean floor, faint contact shadow, neutral daylight-balanced large soft studio lighting, color-accurate fur, cat-eye-level normal-to-short-telephoto camera, full body visible with generous margins.
```

## 禁止项

```text
No collar, clothing, accessories, MacBook, Vision Pro, furniture, props, text, labels or watermark. Avoid generic golden British Shorthair identity, kitten proportions, narrow face, amber or neon eyes, pale beige coat, orange tabby, black stripes, pure black tail tip, long legs, humanized posture, extra limbs, merged paws, duplicate tail, illustration, cartoon or CGI sheen.
```

## 角度规则

- 每次只生成一个角度。
- 身体和头部必须朝向同一角度。
- 转面图保持相同中性站姿、相同光线、相同相机高度和近似画面尺度。
- 目标镜头为正面时，正面站姿母版是唯一最高优先级。
- 目标镜头为侧面或三分之四时，正面母版负责身份，对应转面图负责结构。
