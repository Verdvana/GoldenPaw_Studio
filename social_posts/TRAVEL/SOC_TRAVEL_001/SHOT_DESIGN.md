# SOC_TRAVEL_001 — Four-shot design

The four images should feel like one half-day walk, but each remains an independent L3 generation. Continuity is limited to the approved base outfit, optional short wool coat, small shoulder bag, route progression and natural change in light. Do not use an earlier generated shot as a visual identity or wardrobe input.

## Image 01 — 梧桐树下，刚到南京

- **Story beat:** The walk has just begun; the subject is relaxed and curious rather than posing for a landmark.
- **Camera / framing:** Third-person 3/4 rear view from about 4 m away, eye level, 35–50 mm equivalent, full body with generous tree canopy and sidewalk context. Subject sits slightly off-center, walking into the frame.
- **Pose:** One foot stepping forward, shoulders relaxed, one hand holding the shoulder-bag strap, the other hand loosely near the coat hem. Turn the head back toward the photographer by roughly 30–45 degrees, not a full frontal portrait.
- **Expression:** Small closed-mouth smile, soft eyes, mildly surprised by something on the street; natural and unperformed.
- **Background:** Plane-tree trunks and filtered leaves create a tunnel, with a few indistinct pedestrians and low-rise street façades behind. Keep storefronts anonymous and all signage unreadable.
- **Light:** Late-afternoon sun from image-left/front-left, broken by leaves; warm patches on the pavement, face and coat edge, with cooler shade under the trees. Avoid hard glamour rim light.
- **Image function:** Establish南京秋天 and the subject's walking rhythm; this is the most open, bright image of the carousel.

## Image 02 — 明城墙边，边走边看

- **Story beat:** The city becomes quieter and more historical; the subject is taking in the wall rather than looking at the camera.
- **Camera / framing:** Low-ish side-rear angle from about 5–6 m away, roughly waist height, 50 mm equivalent. Full body in the lower-middle third; wall masonry rises diagonally behind to create depth.
- **Pose:** Walking parallel to the wall, torso angled slightly toward the bricks, one hand lightly grazing the railing or hovering near the wall without theatrical touching. Head turned toward the wall, stride unhurried, coat open enough to show the approved outfit.
- **Expression:** Profile or near-profile with a quiet attentive look; relaxed mouth, slightly narrowed eyes from the sun, no smile required.
- **Background:** Weathered gray brick in clear but not hyper-detailed focus, autumn tree crowns above, a long empty stretch of path and very small distant walkers. No posed tourist groups.
- **Light:** Lower warm sunlight from behind/right, producing a restrained edge on hair and shoulder; the face remains readable in soft reflected light from the path. Keep brick texture neutral gray, not orange.
- **Image function:** Give the carousel its strongest historical texture and a calmer emotional middle beat.

## Image 03 — 新街口夜晚，蹲下来摸一只奶牛猫

- **Story beat:** In the middle of the busy Nanjing evening, the subject pauses for a small, gentle street encounter.
- **Camera / framing:** Third-person candid view from about 3–4 m away, eye level or slightly above the subject's shoulder line, 50 mm equivalent. Use a vertical medium-full shot that keeps the crouched body, both feet, the cat and enough pedestrian-street context in frame. Do not make it a low-angle glamour shot.
- **Pose:** One leg in a normal deep squat with that foot planted flat. The other leg is offset beside it: knee close to the ground, forefoot/toes planted, heel naturally raised. Keep the pelvis, knee and ankle anatomically coherent; no crossed legs or impossible folded joints. One hand reaches down to gently stroke the cat's back/shoulder area, the other hand rests naturally near the thigh for balance.
- **Expression:** Clear adult closed-to-open smile with natural upper teeth visible, bright but unforced; gaze directed at the cat, not the camera.
- **Background:** Nanjing Xinjiekou pedestrian street at night: broad pedestrian paving, layered storefront and building lights, soft traffic/pedestrian movement in the distance, urban density without readable brand names, ads, route signs or fake text. The cat sits at the edge of the walkway rather than in the traffic flow.
- **Light:** Realistic pedestrian-street lighting at night: warm storefront spill mixed with neutral-white overhead street and façade lights, soft pools of light on the pavement, gentle rim on hair and shoulders. Keep the cat and the subject's hands readable without a spotlight or beauty-light effect.
- **Cat:** One recurring TECH_CAT identity, a small black-and-white tuxedo/bicolor cat with the registered face/eyes/nose/ears and natural coat pattern. The cat is calm and receptive, four paws grounded, no costume, collar, leash or invented device.
- **Image function:** Add a warm, spontaneous human-animal moment and a clear nighttime change of pace after the historical wall image.

## Image 04 — 秦淮水岸，天快黑了

- **Story beat:** The walk ends in a quiet pause beside the water; the subject is looking outward, not performing for the camera.
- **Camera / framing:** Third-person medium-long shot from behind and slightly to one side, 5–7 m away, 35–50 mm equivalent. Subject occupies the lower-right or lower-middle area, leaving room for water and dusk reflections.
- **Pose:** Standing naturally beside the stone railing, weight on one leg, the other foot slightly relaxed; both hands resting lightly on the railing or one hand holding the shoulder-bag strap. Head turned toward the water, back and shoulder line clearly readable.
- **Expression:** Face not required; if a small cheek/profile edge appears, keep it calm and contemplative. No direct look to camera.
- **Background:** Calm Qinhuai water, soft reflected lights, dark tree silhouettes and a restrained line of distant architecture. Keep boats, signs and distant text indistinct.
- **Light:** Blue hour ambient fill with warm practical lights reflected on the water; a very soft warm edge on the coat and hair from the riverside side. Avoid neon saturation and overexposed highlights.
- **Image function:** Close the story with breathing room and a quiet aftertaste; visually the darkest and most spacious frame.

## Carousel rhythm

`bright / historical / intimate-night / quiet` is the intended emotional progression. Keep color grading natural across all four: muted autumn greens and gray masonry in images 01–02, then warm storefront amber and neutral-white pedestrian-street light in image 03, with restrained blacks and no orange-teal travel preset. The subject should look like the same person moving through one afternoon into evening, while each frame remains an independently constructed shot.
