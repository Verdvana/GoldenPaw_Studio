# 《打不开的柜门》六格分镜图 v1

Use case: identity-preserve
Asset type: cinematic storyboard/contact sheet for a 20-second vertical short film

Input images:
- Locked female-lead key art: exact woman identity, body, hair and wardrobe.
- Locked cat key art: exact Cyber Golden Shaded identity, face, green eyes, coat and plump body.
- Locked dual scale sheet: human/cat relative size.
- Dual official key-art candidate: shared lighting, relationship mood and photorealistic finish.

Primary request:
- Create one clean horizontal 3-by-2 storyboard sheet with six equal cinematic 9:16-style panels, all set in the same warm modern kitchen/dining storage area.
- Exact same woman and exact same cat in every applicable panel.
- No labels, captions, numbers, speech bubbles or text.

Panel 1:
- Medium-wide shot. Woman crouches at a low light-oak cabinet, places one transparent unlabeled pouch of pale salmon-colored fish treats inside and fastens a small pet-proof latch. A dark smartphone is visibly left on the counter beside the cabinet. Cat watches nearby and tests the closed door once with one front paw; door stays shut.

Panel 2:
- Low-angle close shot beside the cabinet. Cat sits behind a dark-gray electronics mat. Foreground shows a small circuit board sliding into a cat-paw-sized dark-gray one-button remote housing, a faint cyan-green LED illuminated, and a matching tiny actuator. Cat remains realistically feline, no human hands.

Panel 3:
- Low side view of the same cabinet. Tiny actuator mounted beside the inner latch. Cat presses the remote with one paw; latch releases and cabinet door begins opening toward camera-left. Cat looks serious and focused.

Panel 4:
- Reverse view from inside the cabinet past the fish-treat pouch. Cat paw reaches inward. In background, woman has returned for the forgotten smartphone and stops after seeing the cat. They make eye contact and freeze.

Panel 5:
- Medium two-character shot. Cat has withdrawn paw, presses remote to close cabinet, pushes remote aside and sits very straight while looking away. Woman stands behind, quietly suppressing a smile.

Panel 6:
- Warm low-angle closing shot. Woman crouches beside cat, gently pets its head, manually opens same cabinet and places one portion of fish treat in front of it. Cat eats eagerly with head down; woman watches with a tender amused smile.

Continuity:
- Same light-oak single low cabinet, same warm side daylight, same unlabeled fish pouch, same dark smartphone, same dark-gray single-button remote with cyan LED, same tiny actuator and electronics mat.
- Woman always wears blush-pink long-sleeve ribbed top, white A-line mini skirt, white semi-sheer pantyhose, blush-pink rectangular-buckle medium heels, Apple Watch and stud earrings.
- Cat always retains wide round face, full cheeks, olive-green eyes, warm apricot-gold low-contrast coat, cream muzzle/chest and realistic 7–8 kg plump body.

Style:
- Crisp natural photorealistic cinematic storyboard frames, realistic skin, hair, fabric, hosiery, fur, wood, electronics and food packaging; subtle animation-like readability only in gestures.

Avoid:
- identity drift, different room/cabinet between panels, cabinet door opening different direction, changing remote, phone disappearing before panel 4, cat size drift, anthropomorphic standing cat, humanlike cat hands, exaggerated expressions, giant machinery, whole fish/sashimi platter/canned food, extra people/animals/limbs, painterly or smeared colors, text, logos and watermark.

Generation method: Codex built-in image generation.
